Detailed Description

The critters of one wave. Releases them one after the other on the entry, moves them and settles the coins: the reward of every killed critter is added, the coins stolen by every critter that reached the exit are taken. Do not copy the group while towers hold pointers from getActiveCritters. CritterGroup is Observable: adding or removing a critter, and every update in which a critter of the wave spawns, changes cell, loses hit points, dies or escapes, call notify(), so the attached observers (CritterGroupObserver) re-display the wave.
